## EEAT Validation Requirements

Every article must satisfy ALL of the following:

1. Author named in frontmatter (`authorId` field matches a valid key in `src/_data/authors.json`)
2. Author bio card included at the bottom of the article (standard HTML block)
3. Author credentials mentioned in the dateline: "By [Name], [Credentials]"
4. Author bio URL matches the `url` field for that author
5. Article must cite at least one primary regulatory source by name (CBDT, ATO, SEBI, ASIC, PFRDA, APRA, etc.)
6. At least one worked example with a named individual and specific numbers
7. Disclaimer present at the bottom, above the author bio card
8. Published date in frontmatter (`datePublished`)
9. Schema markup: BlogPosting + FAQPage + BreadcrumbList in `<script type="application/ld+json">`
10. At least 5 FAQ items in the faq-list section
11. Internal link to at least one CalcPhi calculator
12. Internal links to at least 2 related CalcPhi articles
13. Word count 900–1,400 words (body text, not counting schema/HTML)
14. No guaranteed return claims ("will earn", "guaranteed to") — use "historically", "subject to market risk"
15. Rates/thresholds cited with effective date or source
16. Title tag under 60 characters in `<title>` (from frontmatter `title` field)
